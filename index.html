We line up at the counter of our breathing,
Exchanging minutes for a hundred things—
A morning commute, a quiet evening,
Or chasing after what tomorrow brings.

We spend without a thought on small distractions,
We waste a quiet fortune on regret,
Investing in our petty, cold reactions,
Forgiving all the debts we should forget.

Yet every heartbeat deducts from the vault,
A silent ledger kept beneath the skin.
We claim our bankruptcy is someone's fault,
Ignoring where the spending must begin.

Remember, time is the only currency you do not know the balance to, spend it wisely for you never know when you become broke.

So trade your precious gold for laughter spoken,
For loving hard, for holding someone near.
Before the account is closed, the thread is broken,
And all your unused wealth turns into fear.
